## Learning outcomes

On completion, students will be able to:

1. Characterize minima, Pareto-optimality, and optimality conditions for constrained and unconstrained problems.
2. Select and apply appropriate line-search and derivative-free / derivative-based algorithms.
3. Handle constrained problems via penalty, barrier, augmented Lagrangian, and quadratic programming methods.
4. Model random phenomena and analyze systems driven by stochastic processes.
5. Derive spectral representations and apply Wiener and Kalman filtering.
6. Analyze discrete- and continuous-time Markov chains and elementary queuing systems.
