# ECEG-6201 — Analytical Optimization and Stochastic Processes

**Degree program:** Master of Science in Communication Engineering  
**Semester:** Year 1, Semester I · **ECTS:** 3 (5) · **Status:** General Area Compulsory

---

## Course objectives

- Familiarize students with engineering optimization methods.
- Equip students with techniques for formulating and solving engineering problems.
- Understand the principles of modelling and analysis of random phenomena.
- Provide the mathematical tools needed to analyze systems affected by random processes.
- Provide the algorithms required for representation and estimation of random signals.
